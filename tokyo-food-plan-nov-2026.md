## Sun 8 Nov — Ginza, then Asakusa + Skytree

**LUNCH · Ginza Rangetsu — 12:00** ✅ **BOOKED & CONFIRMED**
Booked via Ikyu (restaurant.ikyu.com), reached from ginza-rangetsu.com.
Reservation number and auth code are in the Ikyu confirmation email, not here — together they're enough for anyone to cancel the booking.
**Table seating, 4 adults. Seat-only — ¥0 charged, order and pay on the day.**
7 min walk. English-speaking staff. Restaurant: **03-3567-1021**

⚠️ **ARRIVE BY 12:30** — over 30 min late is auto-cancelled.
⚠️ **They may phone to confirm.** If they can't reach you, they may cancel. Make sure your number works on roaming, or give them the Aloft desk (03-6278-8122) as backup.
Free cancellation online until **Fri 6 Nov**; after that phone the restaurant. Silent no-show = no charge, but they may refuse future bookings.
Afternoon: Ginza line runs direct to Asakusa, ~17 min.

*What to order on the day — prices include tax and service:*
- **Sukiyaki & Shabu-Shabu course** ¥8,000 (Plum) / ¥9,000 (Bamboo) ← the pick, gets both preparations
- Shabu-Shabu only ¥8,000 / ¥9,000
- A5 wagyu sets (curry, mizore-yaki, kakuni) ¥2,200–2,600 — the cheap route
- ⚠️ The ¥9,000 "Hana" course is weekday-only, won't be available on a Sunday

❌ Never book Rangetsu via Google Maps — the reseller packages there run ¥15,495–36,155pp, roughly 2–6x the real price.

**DINNER · Sometarō, Asakusa** — walk in
Cook-your-own okonomiyaki in an old wooden house, ~¥2,000pp. English instructions.
**CASH ONLY.** Open till 20:30.
Only day this works — shuts Tue + Wed, no Fri dinner.

## Tue 10 Nov — Ueno Park + Yanaka

**LUNCH · Izuei Honten** — walk in, **ARRIVE 11:00 OPENING**
300-year-old unagi house by Shinobazu Pond. English menu. ~¥4,000 avg lunch.
Go **inside** to the podium and take a ticket number — don't wait outside.
Waits run 10–45 min purely on timing.
Phone booking possible: **03-3831-0954** — phone only, no online.
**Backup — 3 branches within 5 min walk, all open Tue:**
- Shinobazu-tei — ~¥3,240 lunch, English menu
- Umekawa-tei — ~¥6,000, park views, tatami (shoes off, low seating)
⚠️ Avoid 15:00–16:30 on weekdays — menu drops to unaju + kabayaki only.

**DINNER · Ponta Honke, Ueno** — walk in, ~10 min wait
Bib Gourmand tonkatsu since 1905. Open till 20:20.
Skip the fried prawn — market price, can top ¥4,000.
❌ Google Maps routes to OMAKASE which prepays ¥3,300pp (¥13,200), non-refundable. Just walk in.

## Notes

- **Contactless cards work at the gates.** Tokyo Metro, Toei and Keikyu all take a tapped bank card — which covers every journey on this trip, including the airport transfer. JR is the one network that still doesn't, and nothing here needs JR.
- **Suica still worth having** — on the phone or as a card. It pays at konbini, vending machines and lockers where a bank card won't.
- **Carry cash.** Sometarō and most Tsukiji stalls are cash-only.
- All prices JPY, **per person**. Verify at booking — Japanese restaurant prices have risen recently.
- Cancellation in Japan is strict: often 100% same-day, ~50% the day before.
- Every Google Maps booking route except Bird Land pushed toward a pricier product than the walk-in. Google surfaces what the platform monetises.
- Lunch-first is deliberate: same kitchens, roughly half to a fifth of the dinner price.
